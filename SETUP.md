# Running Plan app: setup

About 10 minutes, once. No code skills needed.

## 1. Put the plan in Google Docs

1. Upload the Word copy of the plan (`Running Plan.docx`) to Google Drive. Then **right-click it → Open with → Google Docs**, and in Google Docs choose **File → Save as Google Docs**. (If the Running Plan doc in Claude has an export to Google Drive option, that works too.)
2. In the Google Doc, click **Share**. Under **General access**, choose **Anyone with the link**, role **Viewer**. Click **Copy link**, then **Done**.

Keep that link private: anyone who has it can read the plan. It never goes in this repository.

## 2. Turn on the app's web address (GitHub Pages)

1. On GitHub, open this repository, then **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch that holds the app (normally **main**) and **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
3. After 1–2 minutes the page shows **"Your site is live at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/Training/"**.

## 3. Install it on your phone

1. Open that address in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
2. Go to **Settings** in the app, paste the Google Doc link, and tap **Save**. The plan appears. The link is saved on this phone only.
3. Add it to the home screen: Chrome **⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install**; Safari **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Day to day

- **Today** shows today's workout, which shoe to wear, the next race countdown and the next three days.
- **Plan** lists every week. Tap one to see all its days.
- **Guide** has the rest of the doc (paces, shoes, drills, rules), folded by section.
- **Changing the plan:** edit the Google Doc. The app picks it up the next time it opens, or tap **Settings → Check the plan for changes**.
- **No signal:** the app shows the last copy it loaded.
- **"Google won't share this doc"** in Settings means the doc's sharing isn't set to "Anyone with the link".
